# A Realization of F4 for Litt Problem No. 13

I construct a complex abelian fourfold and an integral surface whose full convolution Tannaka group is the simple group of type F4. I begin with the Fano surface of lines on a smooth cubic threefold with an Eckardt point, quotient its Albanese variety by the elliptic curve of lines through that point, and identify the Tannaka group of the resulting intersection complex.
