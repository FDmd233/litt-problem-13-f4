# A Realization of F4 for Litt Problem No. 13

This repository contains a construction of a complex abelian fourfold and an integral surface whose full convolution Tannaka group is the simple group of type (F_4). The construction starts from the Fano surface of lines on a smooth cubic threefold with an Eckardt point, quotients its Albanese variety by the elliptic curve of lines through that point, and identifies the Tannaka group of the resulting intersection complex.
