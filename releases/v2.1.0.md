# Revised $F_4$ manuscript — proof correction (21 October 2026)

This release supersedes `v2.0.0` as the **current version of the proposed $F_4$ realization** for [Litt Problem #13](https://www.problemsilike.com/13). Historical releases are preserved.

## Mathematical statement in the manuscript

From a smooth cubic threefold with a marked Eckardt point, the manuscript considers the Fano surface $F$, its Albanese variety $A$, the elliptic curve $E\subset A$, the quotient $B=A/E$, and the image surface $X\subset B$. The principal claims are

```math
Rf_*\mathbb C_F[2]\cong\operatorname{IC}_X\oplus\delta_0,
\qquad
\bigl(G(\operatorname{IC}_X),\omega(\operatorname{IC}_X)\bigr)
 \cong(F_4,V_{26}).
```
